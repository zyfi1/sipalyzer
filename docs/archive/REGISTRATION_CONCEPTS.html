Registration View Concepts

Seven distinct layout approaches for presenting SIP registrars. Each concept prioritizes different trade-offs: density vs clarity, at-a-glance status vs detail depth, flat lists vs grouped views.

Concept A

Status Cards

Each registrar gets its own card with a subtle status glow. Great for visual scanning when you have fewer registrars (<15). Status-colored gradient wash makes state immediately obvious.

Pros: Very visual. Status is unmissable. Each registrar has clear breathing room. Easy to add richer data per card without crowding.
Cons: Doesn't scale well past ~12 registrars. Uses a lot of vertical space. Harder to compare across registrars side-by-side.
Best for: Users with fewer registrars who want a dashboard-like feel. Could combine with a compact list toggle.
Concept B

Dense Table

Classic data-table with sticky headers, sortable columns, and inline actions on hover. Maximum information density — fit 20+ registrars on screen without scrolling.

Best for: Power users managing many registrars who want all data at a glance without expanding anything.

Best for: SIP-savvy users who care about the handshake flow. Could be a toggle within another concept.
Concept D

Split Panel

Sidebar list + detail pane. Clicking a registrar reveals rich detail: stats, history, raw SIP, and actions. The sidebar stays compact while the detail area has room to breathe.

Pros: Beautiful, calming, modern. Actions don't clutter unless needed. Focus on names and status. Scales well vertically. Very readable.

Best for: Users who value aesthetics and simplicity. Pairs well with keyboard shortcuts for power users. Good default with a "show more" expansion.

Pros: No folder navigation needed — everything on one page. Aggregate badges per group. Clean row design with left-bar status accent. Collapsible groups reduce clutter.

Best for: Users who have organized registrars into folders and want a single-page overview without clicking between folder views.
